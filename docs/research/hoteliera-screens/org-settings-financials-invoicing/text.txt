Auto-issue invoice on online payment (MobilPay/PayPal)

When a guest pays online via MobilPay or PayPal, automatically issue a fiscal invoice for the amount paid and attach the payment to it. Works with and without proforma.

Final invoice mode
Instalment billing, no final invoice
Each instalment invoices the services it covers, so nothing needs reversing at the end. No consolidating final invoice is offered.
Separate storno invoices
Each prior invoice is reversed by its own storno document, then a final invoice is issued for the full amount of the stay.
Reversals inside the final invoice
No separate storno documents. The final invoice lists the full amount of the stay plus one negative line per prior invoice, so it shows only what is still owed, or closes at zero when the prior invoices already cover everything.
